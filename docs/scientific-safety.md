# Scientific Safety

Open Drug Lab is educational research software. It helps learners run small,
reproducible computational chemistry workflows and understand the resulting
artifacts.

It does not:

- discover drugs
- predict clinical safety or efficacy
- provide medical advice
- provide toxicity, dosing, regulatory, or treatment recommendations
- replace review by qualified computational chemists, medicinal chemists,
  toxicologists, clinicians, or regulatory experts

## Report Language

Reports should describe outputs as screening signals, descriptors, flags, or
educational observations. Avoid language such as:

- "safe"
- "effective"
- "approved"
- "drug candidate"
- "cure"
- "clinically relevant"

Prefer language such as:

- "triggered a simple rule flag"
- "requires expert review"
- "educational descriptor summary"
- "not a safety or efficacy prediction"

## Example Data

Default examples should use familiar small molecules for teaching. Avoid
positioning examples as active therapeutic discovery.
